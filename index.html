📚 BS 5950-1:2000 Design Code References

  • Fillet Weld Capacity (Transverse & Longitudinal): Derived from Clause 6.8.7.3 (Directional Method). Design strength \(p_w = 220 \text{ N/mm}^2\) from Table 37.
  • Fillet Weld Throat Thickness: \(a = 0.7 \times \text{Leg Length}\) per Clause 6.8.3.
  • Base Plate Bending: Compression side bending follows the effective area method from Clause 4.13.2.2.
  • Concrete Bearing Strength: Taken as \(0.6 f_{cu}\) (or grout strength, whichever is weaker).
  • Shear Transfer: Friction coefficient \(\mu = 0.30\) is standard industry practice (BS 5950 does not strictly dictate this, but it is standard for steel on concrete).
